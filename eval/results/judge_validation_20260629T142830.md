# Legal Status Change of Codeine Linctus

**How it changed:**
Codeine linctus has been reclassified from a pharmacy-only medicine (P) to a prescription-only medicine (POM). This means it will no longer be available without a prescription.

**Why it changed:**
The reclassification was made due to:

1. **Risk of dependence and addiction** – Codeine linctus is an opioid medicine known to be addictive, and addiction can happen gradually, especially with long-term use.

2. **Recreational misuse** – Codeine linctus has been used recreationally in drinks known as "purple drank" (also called "lean," "sizzurp," or "dirty sprite"), which carries serious risks of addiction and overdose that can be fatal.

3. **Overdose risk** – Users of recreational codeine drinks may not be aware of how much they are consuming, leading to risks such as loss of consciousness, respiratory suppression, and death. These risks are further increased when combined with central nervous system depressants like alcohol or sedatives.

4. **Increased illicit sales** – The MHRA received an increased number of reports of codeine linctus being sold through non-regulated and potentially illicit websites.

5. **Safety monitoring data** – Deaths involving codeine increased from 88 in 2011 to 200 in 2021.

The reclassification is a risk minimisation measure to protect patient health, prevent recreational use, and enable identification of individuals who may have become unintentionally addicted.

- Intense itching and burning
- Cycles of flaking skin
